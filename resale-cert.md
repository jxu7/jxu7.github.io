## ⚠️ The Big One: Reserve Fund Is Only 25% Funded
This is the most important thing in the whole packet, and I want to make sure it doesn't get lost in the shuffle:

- **Fully funded balance should be ~$19,023,243.** Actual reserve balance is only **$4,750,000** — a **~$14.3 million deficit**, or about **$83,469 per unit** in theoretical shortfall.

- **Why it matters for you:** low reserve funding is one of the most common reasons condo buildings hit owners with unexpected special assessments. It doesn't mean one is coming, but it's the main financial risk in this packet, and it's also something mortgage lenders (especially for Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac-backed loans) sometimes flag or require extra documentation for.
